# Manifesto

1. **Augmentation Over Automation** — AI should enhance human strengths rather than replace them.

2. **Adaptability Over Rigid Programming** — AI must adapt to evolving environments and needs.

3. **Transparency Over Black-Box Operation** — AI systems should operate transparently to foster trust and accountability.

4. **Human-Centered Design Over Technical Efficiency** — AI must be designed with human needs and natural workflows in mind.

5. **Collaborative Intelligence Over Isolated Operation** — AI should support collaboration between humans, other systems, and agents.

6. **Responsible Operation Over Unrestricted Capabilities** — AI must operate ethically, with safeguards to prevent harm and bias.

7. **Continuous Improvement Over Static Deployment** — AI should evolve and improve continuously, staying relevant over time.

Source: George Vossos, [The AI Agents Manifesto](https://medium.com/@gvossos/the-ai-agents-manifesto-a-new-framework-for-human-ai-collaboration-90542f820266).
