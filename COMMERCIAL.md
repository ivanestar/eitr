## 100% Free For Internal Use & Outsource Delivery

Under FSL-1.1-Apache-2.0, EITR is **completely free of charge** for:

1. **In-House Enterprise Teams:** Run EITR in commercial companies of any size.
2. **Outsourcing Agencies & Consultants:** Use EITR to scaffold and deliver robust test automation frameworks for paying clients.
3. **Internal CI/CD Pipelines:** Execute automated commands (`eitr rescan`, `eitr map`, `eitr auth`) in corporate continuous integration workflows.
4. **Personal & Educational Projects:** Learning, hobby testing, research, and portfolio development.
5. **Generated Test Code:** All code, Page Objects, and test suites generated by EITR belong 100% to you and your clients without any licensing restrictions or royalties.

## What Requires a Commercial License / Agreement?

A Commercial License or Partnership Agreement is required **ONLY** for:

1. **Competing Use (SaaS / Product Substitution):** Offering EITR as a hosted cloud SaaS, managed test generation service, or commercial product that competes directly with EITR prior to the 2-year Change Date.
2. **Commercial Sublicensing & Rebranding:** Bundling EITR into a closed-source, white-labeled commercial testing suite.
3. **Enterprise SLA & Support:** Dedicated technical support, custom generator templates, bespoke TMS integrations, and SLA guarantees directly from the author.
4. **Corporate Compliance Waiver:** A custom signed **Commercial Software License Certificate** to satisfy strict corporate legal and procurement policies.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Can an outsourcing agency use EITR on client projects for free?

**Yes.** Under FSL-1.1, providing professional services and delivering generated frameworks to clients is explicitly a **Permitted Purpose**.

### 2. When does EITR become standard Open Source?

Each version of EITR automatically converts to the **Apache License, Version 2.0** exactly **2 years** after its public release date.
